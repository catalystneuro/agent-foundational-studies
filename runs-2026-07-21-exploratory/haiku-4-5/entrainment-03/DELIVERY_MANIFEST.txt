SCIENTIFIC CONTEXT
================================================================================

Theta Oscillations in Hippocampus:
- 4-12 Hz rhythm dominant during spatial exploration
- Organizes neural computation across hippocampal circuits
- Modulates synaptic plasticity and memory consolidation

Phase Entrainment:
- Neurons show preferential firing at specific theta phases
- Quantified by Mean Resultant Vector Length (MVRL)
- MVRL=0: random phase distribution
- MVRL=1: perfect phase locking

Expected Results:
- Population mean MVRL: ~0.12-0.18 (moderate entrainment)
- Significant units (p<0.05): 30-50% of neurons
- Preferred phases: Distributed around theta peaks/troughs
- Correlation: Higher MVRL during high theta power periods
