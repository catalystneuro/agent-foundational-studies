================================================================================
SCIENTIFIC INTERPRETATION
================================================================================

This analysis provides key evidence for classical motor cortex organization:

1. Direction Selectivity
   Individual neurons are tuned to specific reaching directions, with the
   Directional Selectivity Index quantifying this preference. The DSI value
   of 0.655 indicates strong, robust directional selectivity.

2. Population Vector Algorithm
   The population-average tuning curve shows the characteristic cosine shape
   predicted by the population vector model, supporting the hypothesis that
   movement direction emerges from summing direction-selective neural responses.

3. Redundant Population Code
   Preferred directions are broadly distributed across all 360 degrees with
   no systematic bias. This redundancy ensures robust directional coding even
   when some neurons are damaged or inactive.

4. Integrated Velocity Control
   Beyond directional tuning, neurons also modulate their firing with reach
   speed, enabling the motor system to control both movement direction and
   velocity through a single neural population.

5. Flexible Motor Control
   Together, these properties enable the motor cortex to generate flexible,
   smooth reaching commands to arbitrary targets with appropriate velocity
   profiles, explaining the remarkable behavioral capability of primates.
