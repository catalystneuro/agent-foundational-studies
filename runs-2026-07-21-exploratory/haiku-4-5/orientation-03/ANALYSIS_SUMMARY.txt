================================================================================
SCIENTIFIC BACKGROUND
================================================================================

What is Orientation Selectivity?
  Neurons in primary visual cortex respond preferentially to visual edges
  of particular orientations. Individual neurons have a "preferred
  orientation" - the angle that elicits maximum response.

Why is it Important?
  - Fundamental feature of visual cortex circuitry
  - Enables efficient representation of visual edges/contours
  - Important for object recognition and scene understanding
  - Emergent property arising from cortical connectivity

How is it Measured?
  1. Present visual gratings at multiple orientations
  2. Record neural responses (spike times)
  3. Compute average firing rate per orientation
  4. Fit tuning curves (typically von Mises functions)
  5. Calculate selectivity indices:
     - Preferred orientation: angle of peak response
     - OSI (0-1): strength of orientation preference
     - Tuning width: sharpness of response curve

Population Properties:
  - Orientations are distributed across all angles
  - Different neurons prefer different orientations
  - Creates a columnar organization in cortex
  - Provides redundant yet distributed visual representation
