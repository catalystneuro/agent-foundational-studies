AUDITORY FREQUENCY TUNING ANALYSIS - COMPLETED
================================================

Dataset Source: DANDI Archive dandiset 000986
Publication: Papadopoulos et al. (2025) in Neuron
Data: Mouse primary auditory cortex (A1) recordings using Neuropixels probes

Session analyzed: LA11, Session 2
- 132 single units recorded
- 7,448 stimulus trials
- 5 frequency levels: 2, 4, 8, 16, 32 kHz

KEY FINDINGS:
- 77% of units responsive to auditory stimuli (mean firing > 1 Hz)
- Preferred frequencies distributed across population with skew toward lower frequencies
- 37.6% of units prefer 2 kHz, 25.7% prefer 8 kHz
- Moderate frequency selectivity (Q10 ~0.49 on average)
- Population tuning curve peaks at 8 kHz with broad tuning across frequency range
