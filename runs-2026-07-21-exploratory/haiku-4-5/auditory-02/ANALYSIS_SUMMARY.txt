   - Optimizes encoding of natural acoustic spectra

4. POPULATION CODING
   - Overlapping tuning curves enable fine frequency discrimination
   - Population provides robust representation with noise tolerance
   - Bandwidth and spacing reflect trade-off between selectivity and smoothness
