# Hippocampal Place Cells (DANDI :001754)

This analysis demonstrates the classic hippocampal place-cell phenomenon using data
streamed directly from the DANDI Archive: [Dandiset 001754](https://dandiarchive.org/dandiset/001754),
"Three-dimensional spatial selectivity of hippocampal neurons during space flight"
(Knierim, McNaughton & Poe). The dandiset contains tetrode recordings from hippocampal
area CA1 in three rats aboard the Neurolab Space Shuttle mission (STS-90, April 1998),
with sessions that interleave a flat, two-dimensional "Baseline" foraging task with the
study's specialized 3-D "Escher Staircase" and "Magic Carpet" tasks. We used
`sub-Rat1/sub-Rat1_ses-19980425T124500_behavior+ecephys.nwb` (Rat 1, Flight Day 9), whose
~44-minute in-flight baseline epoch is the longest continuous 2-D foraging recording in
the dandiset and gave the most statistically robust place fields of the sessions we
inspected.

The data were streamed with `remfile` (no full-file download) and loaded with `pynapple`.
Position tracking required two cleaning steps before it could be used: removing a
video-tracker error code (a fixed out-of-arena coordinate emitted whenever the head-stage
LED was briefly lost) and collapsing repeated, bit-identical position samples, which arise
because position in this dataset was resampled onto pooled spike timestamps rather than a
fixed video frame rate (a zero-order hold). After restricting to locomotion epochs (speed
> 3 px/s), we computed occupancy-normalized 2-D firing-rate maps for all 39 recorded
units, excluded bins with under a second of real dwell time from the statistics (fleeting
visits produce unreliable, noise-dominated rate estimates), and quantified spatial tuning
with Skaggs spatial information (bits/spike). Significance was assessed against a
per-unit null distribution built from 500 circular shifts of each unit's spike train.

Two of 36 well-sampled units reached significance (p < 0.05) against the shuffle null,
with one more trending toward significance, consistent with genuine hippocampal place
cells: their firing is concentrated in a restricted region of the arena well beyond what
spike count and sampling density would produce by chance. The clearest example, unit 10
(55 spikes, p = 0.008), fires almost exclusively in one corner of the arena that the
animal also visits from multiple directions, the textbook signature of a place field.
Because this baseline epoch was recorded in-flight, this place-tuned firing was expressed
by the hippocampus of a rat navigating in microgravity, illustrating that this basic
spatial coding mechanism survives outside Earth's normal gravitational and vestibular
context.
